Red Moon Inn environment: what the conversion left out

From environment/red_moon_inn, 8e0929053dd7ecd0.patch_024 in resource_packages/levels/inn.

Setting waystone_environment_01, which Vermintide 2 looks for, is a copy of default.
light_shaft_enabled is light_shafts_enabled in Vermintide 2.
light_shaft_settings is light_shafts_settings in Vermintide 2.
light_shaft_weigth is light_shafts_weigth in Vermintide 2.
sun_shadow_slice_depth_ranges is the keep's: Vermintide 1 has it in other units.
sun_world_to_shadow_slice0, sun_world_to_shadow_slice1, sun_world_to_shadow_slice2, sun_world_to_shadow_slice3 are left to the engine, which works them out.
