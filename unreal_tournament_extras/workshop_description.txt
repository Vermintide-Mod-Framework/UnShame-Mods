Cheats, tests and dev commands for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3814159978]Unreal Tournament Weapons & Movement[/url].

[h1]Chat Commands[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]/god[/b] - take no damage.
[*][b]/allammo[/b] - fill your ammo.
[*][b]/fly[/b], [b]/ghost[/b] - fly where you look, through walls with ghost. [b]/walk[/b] ends both.
[*][b]/suicide[/b] - die at once.
[*][b]/addbots[/b], [b]/killbots[/b] - add or remove hero bots.
[*][b]/hud[/b] - hide the HUD, crosshair and weapon. [b]/hud 1[/b] keeps the weapon.
[*][b]/freecamera[/b] - a third person camera you can turn around your character.
[*][b]/slomo[/b] - change the game speed. 0 stops time.
[*][b]/recharge[/b] - fill your career skill and adrenaline.
[/list]

[h1]Development[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]/ut_level[/b] - load any level, with an optional weather and difficulty.
[*][b]/ut_test[/b] - run the in-game tests of the weapons.
[*][b]/ut_equip[/b] - equip a test's weapon, or put your loadout back.
[*][b]/ut_quiet[/b] - keep the level empty and yourself unhurt, as the tests do.
[*][b]/ut_spawn[/b] - spawn an enemy ahead of you.
[*][b]/ut_tp[/b] - move to a point.
[*][b]/ut_where[/b] - show where you stand.
[*][b]/ut_probe[/b] - show how far the level is above and around you.
[*][b]/ut_3p[/b] - switch the third person view on or off.
[*][b]/ut_skin[/b] - inspect and adjust a weapon's model.
[/list]
